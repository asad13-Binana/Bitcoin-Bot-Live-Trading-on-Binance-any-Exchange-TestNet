BITCOIN SPOT BOT — PLAIN DESCRIPTION

This bot watches and trades one Bitcoin Spot pair at a time, such as BTC/USDT or
BTC/USDC. You choose the permitted stable quote. It never searches for other coins.

Freqtrade calculates the supplied 1-minute entry signal and its 5-minute trend filter.
A separate read-only service watches Spot order-book pressure, recent aggressive buys
and sells, and the same-symbol USD-M market when it exists. It also reports 15-minute,
1-hour, 2-hour, 4-hour, and 1-day context. Those extra charts do not secretly change
the inherited signal formula.

Only the execution sidecar holds Binance keys. It records what it intends to do before
calling Binance, checks current exchange rules, and refuses to guess after a timeout.
Entries start OFF. Telegram lets only the configured owner resume/pause, inspect status,
switch a verified-flat BTC pair, choose OCO/trailing protection, request break-even or
profit-lock protection, reconcile, and perform a confirmed emergency exit.

Simulation is the default. Testnet is the required next step. Live mode cannot start
until the exact build, exact active pair, exact risk settings, and an actual passing
Freqtrade ZIP have been signed with a private key that remains off the server.

This is not a promise of profit. A passing automated audit proves specific software
properties; it does not prove that the strategy has an edge or that an order will fill
during an exchange outage, network failure, or violent gap.
